    What works, and what does not
    
        
    
          
    NTFS, FAT12/16/32, exFATDeleted files, folder tree, ratings, restore
    
          
    ext2/3/4Deleted files through the JBD2 journal while it still holds them
    
          
    APFS, HFS+Detection only — deleted-file recovery is not implemented. Carve instead.
    
          
    Signature carving44 formats, 28 validated; resumable
    
          
    Fragment reassemblySome fragmented JPEG/MP4/PNG files, not most
    
          
    SSDs with TRIMDeleted data is usually already erased by the drive
    
          
    Android over USBTrashed media and caches; not verified on a real phone yet
    
          
    iOS backupsUnencrypted backups only; encrypted ones are refused
    
          
    Phone internal storage, block levelNot possible: file-based encryption
    
        
    
        
    The full list is docs/LIMITATIONS.md.
