Android over USB

  1. Unlock the phone and keep it unlocked.
  2. Turn on USB debugging (Settings › About phone › tap Build number 7 times, then Developer options).
  3. Plug it in and tap Allow on the phone.

      What works, and what does not
      
            
      
              
      
                
      NTFS, FAT, exFATFull Deleted files with their names, folders, dates and content.
      
                
      ext2/3/4 (Linux)Full Through the journal, while it still holds the deletion.
      
                
      APFS, HFS+ (Mac)Detected only Names cannot be recovered; carving still finds files.
      
                
      Carving by signature44 formats 28 of them are verified by decoding, not just a header match.
      
                
      Split (fragmented) filesPartly Some JPEG, PNG and MP4 files are rebuilt; most fragmented files are not.
      
                
      SSDs and NVMe drivesUsually nothing The drive erases deleted data itself, often within seconds.
      
                
      Android internal storageTrash & caches Deleted files are encrypted with keys that are destroyed — no tool recovers them.
      
                
      Android memory cardFull Take the card out and scan it here.
      
                
      iPhoneBackups Unencrypted backups only; "Recently Deleted" photos and their thumbnails.
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              The measured details are in docs/LIMITATIONS.md next to the program.
